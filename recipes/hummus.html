Hummus

Description

Hummus is a smooth, creamy Middle Eastern dip made by blending cooked chickpeas with tahini, olive oil, lemon juice, and garlic. Celebrated worldwide as both a nutritious snack and a versatile spread, it features a rich, nutty flavor profile with a bright, tangy finish that complements everything from warm pita bread and crisp vegetables to grilled meats.

Ingredients

  • Chickpeas
  • Tahini
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Puree the Base
  2. Add the Chickpeas
  3. Adjust the Texture
  4. Season and Serve
